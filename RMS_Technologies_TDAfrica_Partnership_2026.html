04 — Why This Beats Building In-House

Six reasons we don't
build the tech team.

The alternative to TD Africa is hiring engineers, holding stock, financing inventory, and running RMA. Every one of those is a million-naira problem. The partnership removes all of them.

💸
Zero capital required

No inventory holding, no warehouse, no working-capital line, no manufacturer credit terms to negotiate. Every deal funds itself. A 200-laptop order does not need RMS to pre-finance ₦100m of stock.

👥
Zero technical headcount

No need to hire engineers, technicians, deployment teams, or warehouse staff in Year 1. RMS stays a 6-person sales-and-services firm while delivering at the capacity of a 300-person integrator.

📦
Instant product range

Day-one access to every brand TD Africa carries — HP, Dell, Lenovo, Cisco, Microsoft, Apple, Samsung, APC, Bosch, Starlink, Huawei. Building that range from scratch would take 2–3 years and ₦200m+ of relationship work.

🏛️
Enterprise & gov-grade credibility

Federal ministries, banks, and corporates will not buy hardware from an unknown small firm. "Sourced via TD Africa" closes the credibility gap — TD Africa is a name every Nigerian procurement officer recognises.

🎯
Focus stays on the core

RMS does what it does best — sales, account management, M365 deployment, NDPR compliance. Logistics, RMA, warranty, and supply chain are someone else's problem by design.

🌍
West African reach

TD Africa operates in eight countries. A Lagos-based RMS team can credibly propose deployments in Ghana, Kenya, Côte d'Ivoire, and Cameroon — the regional expansion path that funds Year 3.

08 — Partnership Agreement Structure

What the contract
locks in.

The terms of the Authorised Channel Partner agreement. We negotiate from this template and lock the relationship in writing — every commission, every payment cycle, every exclusivity boundary documented.

// Term 01
Channel Partner status

RMS Technologies Limited is recognised as an Authorised Channel Partner of TD Africa, with non-exclusive rights to refer and contract end clients across Nigeria, with optional extension to West Africa.

// Term 02
Commission schedule

Commission rates per the published TD Africa partner schedule (3–18% by category, documented in Section 05 of this document). Reviewed annually; volume tiers earn rebates above thresholds.

// Term 03
Payment & settlement

Hardware commissions settled within 30 days of TD Africa receiving full client payment. Recurring Microsoft licensing commissions settled monthly. All settlements in NGN unless otherwise agreed.

// Term 04
Client ownership

RMS owns the client relationship for any deal it originates. TD Africa is contractually prevented from approaching the client directly for any product within RMS's catalog scope during the agreement period.

// Term 05
Co-branding & marketing

RMS may use "TD Africa Authorised Channel Partner" in marketing materials, proposals, and pitch decks. Joint marketing campaigns (events, webinars, vendor demos) co-funded by both parties on agreed initiatives.

// Term 06
Deal protection

Once RMS registers a deal in TD Africa's CRM, the deal is protected for 90 days against other resellers in the channel. Renewable on demonstrated active engagement with the client.

// Term 07
Training & enablement

RMS sales and technical staff receive free access to TD Africa's vendor training programmes — Microsoft, HP, Dell, Cisco certifications and product enablement. Material commercial value at no additional cost.

// Term 08
Term & exit

Initial three-year term with rolling annual renewal. Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice. Open deals continue to settlement post-termination. No exclusivity penalties.
