01 — Onboarding Philosophy

Speed is the
product.

Six principles govern every onboarding. They are the difference between a client who hits the 12-month renewal and a client who churns at month 3. The first 30 days set the lifetime trajectory of the relationship.

// 01
Five business days, not five months

Incumbent integrators take 8–12 weeks to deploy what we deploy in 5 days. Velocity is a commercial weapon and the brand promise — protect the SLA fiercely.

// 02
MD attends Day 1 and Day 5

The CEO/MD personally attends every kickoff and every handover, regardless of deal size. Senior accountability is the retention insurance policy we built the brand on.

// 03
One named owner per client

Every client is assigned a named Account Manager on Day 1. The AM is the single human the client thinks of when anything goes wrong. No round-robin support queues, ever.

// 04
NDPR is the default

Every onboarding ships with the NDPR documentation pack signed. We do not "offer" NDPR — we include it as standard, because NITDA enforcement makes it table stakes.

// 05
The portal is the deliverable

A Power BI client portal showing licence count, security posture, ticket queue, and NDPR status is the tangible artifact that justifies the monthly invoice. Set it up on Day 5, never skip.

// 06
Documentation lives forever

Every credential, every configuration, every decision documented in the client's run-book stored in 1Password + SharePoint. Bus-factor of one is unacceptable. The client should never be hostage to one engineer's memory.

06 — The NDPR Documentation Pack

Seven documents.
Signed on Day 1.

Every client receives the same seven NDPR documents on Day 1, customised to their entity name and business sector. The pack is the same pack we sell as a standalone NDPR audit service — included free with every managed engagement, because NITDA enforcement makes compliance non-optional.

📋Privacy Policy (public-facing)

Customer-facing policy for the client's website and product. Covers data collection, use, retention, sharing, and subject rights under NDPR. Templated · customised in 2 hours per client.

🔒Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Between client and RMS Technologies as data processor. Required by NDPR Article 28 equivalent. Limits liability, defines processing scope, and documents sub-processors. Signed mutually on Day 1.

📝Internal Data Protection Policy

The client's internal policy governing how staff handle personal data. Covers retention, access control, sharing, and disposal. Annexed to employee handbook.

⚡Breach Response Plan

Step-by-step response plan for a data breach. Defines roles, NITDA notification timeline (72 hours), subject notification, and forensic preservation. Tested annually via tabletop exercise.

📊Data Inventory & Mapping

Documents every category of personal data the client holds: source, location, lawful basis, retention period, recipients. Required for NITDA audit · refreshed annually.

✅Consent Management Framework

Standard consent forms and opt-in flows for marketing, employment, and customer onboarding contexts. Aligned with NDPR consent requirements (specific, informed, freely given).

🛡️Subject Rights Request Procedure

Workflow for handling subject access, deletion, correction, and portability requests. Includes templated response letters and timelines (30 days per NDPR). Lives in client portal.

📄Annual NDPR Compliance Audit

12-month review of the framework against NITDA enforcement guidance. Filed to NITDA where required by client's data-processing threshold. RMS files on behalf of client at no extra cost.

09 — What Goes Wrong · Playbook

Six failures.
Six responses.

The six most common onboarding failures and the documented response to each. Read these before the first onboarding. The reason we hit Day 5 every time is that we know what tends to break and we route around it before it costs the SLA.

User list delivered late or incomplete
Client promises the user list at end of Day 1 but it arrives Day 3 with missing fields. Or arrives in the wrong format and needs rework. The single most common Day 2 blocker.
Pre-populate from LinkedIn during kickoff
During the kickoff meeting itself, the AM opens the client's LinkedIn company page and pre-populates the user list live with everyone visible. Client just adds emails, mobile numbers, and any private hires. Cuts the user-list delay from 2 days to 0.
DNS access blocked or unknown
Client doesn't have access to their domain registrar or doesn't know who manages DNS. Common with older companies where the original web developer holds the registrar credentials.
Day 1 has a "DNS verification" gate
Before leaving Day 1 kickoff, Tech Lead opens MX Toolbox live and verifies the client can reach their DNS. If not, kickoff is paused and the AM works the registrar recovery in parallel. We do not start Day 2 without DNS confirmed accessible.
MFA enrolment resistance from users
"This is annoying, I don't have a smartphone, why do I need this." Mid-level managers especially resist MFA on cultural grounds. Threatens 100% coverage by Day 3.
MD-signed all-hands email on Day 0
The client signatory (CEO/MD on their side) sends an internal email on Day 0 stating MFA enrolment is mandatory and personally signed off. Top-down legitimacy resolves 90% of friction. RMS provides the template; client just signs and sends.
Email migration overruns and Day 4 starts behind
Large mailboxes (executive accounts with 50GB+) take longer than projected. Migration still running when MFA enrolment is meant to start. Threatens Day 5 go-live.
Two-pass migration · executives first
Run the executive group separately on Day 2 evening (smaller batch, complete by morning). General staff run Day 3 evening as planned. Executive mailboxes never determine the Day 5 critical path.
Internal IT contact stalls the project
Client has an internal IT person who feels threatened by RMS arriving. Withholds passwords, asks repeated clarifying questions, drags out scope conversations. Slows everything by 2× silently.
MD-to-MD handoff · explicitly co-opt internal IT
MD escalates to the client signatory: internal IT person becomes the named "client IT lead" with RMS as their delivery partner, not their replacement. Reframe protects the relationship and removes the political resistance.
Day 5 acceptance unsigned · billing blocked
Go-live meeting goes well but the signatory wants "one more week to test before signing acceptance." Billing remains in limbo. Cash flow delayed by an unspecified period.
Pre-frame acceptance in the SOW
The SOW signed at sale time states "go-live acceptance presumed if not contested within 5 business days of Day 5 walkthrough". Day 5 walkthrough is documented in the PSA with screenshots. Billing activates automatically on Day 10 with or without signature.
