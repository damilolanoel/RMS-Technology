01 — Overview

What the CSP programme is —
and why we are running it.

Microsoft's official channel for reselling cloud products. As an Indirect Reseller, we sit between Microsoft (via a distributor) and the end client — owning the relationship while the distributor handles billing.

// The CSP thesis in one paragraph

The Cloud Solution Provider programme is Microsoft's official channel for reselling Microsoft 365, Azure, Dynamics 365, Copilot, and Defender. As an Indirect Reseller, RMS Technologies signs up under a distributor — Westcon-Comstor Africa, Ingram Micro, or Dynamics Africa — who handles the direct Microsoft relationship and billing. RMS provisions, sells, and supports licences to clients, earning a reseller margin on every seat every month. Because Nigeria sits in Microsoft's Africa CSP region, we can sell to clients across 18 African countries from a single enrolment. The economics are simple: zero inventory, zero COGS, recurring monthly cash flow, and a permanent commercial reason to be inside every client's IT environment.

04 — CSP Enrolment

Becoming a CSP Indirect Reseller
in ~10 business days.

The exact sequence. Each step has an owner (CEO until first hire), a portal, and a hard exit criterion. We do not move on until the current step's confirmation email is in our inbox.

01
Day 1
Create a Microsoft work account (M365 tenant)

Sign up for a Microsoft 365 Business trial at microsoft.com/en-ng/microsoft-365 using the @rmstech.ng domain. This creates the Azure AD "work account" required for Partner Center. We can either spin a separate tenant exclusively for partner ops, or use the same tenant we use for internal email — recommendation is one shared tenant on rmstech.ng.

→ Prerequisite: rmstech.ng domain registered and verified. → Exit: work account created, MFA enabled on CEO login.
02
Day 1
Join the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Programme (MAICPP)

Go to partner.microsoft.com → click "Join now" → sign in with the work account from Step 1. Complete the registration form with the CAC-registered company details (legal name, address, phone). A Microsoft Partner Network (MPN ID) is issued within minutes. Save this ID — it is required at every subsequent step. The membership itself is free.

→ Exit: MPN ID issued and saved to the CEO's password manager + a backup copy in 1Password shared vault.
03
Day 1–2
Enrol as CSP Indirect Reseller in Partner Center

Go to partnercenter.microsoft.com → Account settings → Enrol → Select "Cloud Solution Provider" → Select "Indirect Reseller." Fill in legal entity details, support contact info (the email and phone number clients will reach support on), and accept the Microsoft Partner Agreement (MPA). Microsoft verification takes 3–5 business days. Verification email arrives to the global admin account.

→ Critical: the support phone and email shown to Microsoft will be shown to clients. Use a dedicated support@rmstech.ng inbox and a phone number that is always answered during business hours.
04
Week 1–2
Choose and connect with a distributor

A CSP Indirect Reseller cannot transact without an approved distributor. We will engage all three options in parallel, then commit to one as primary. The distributor handles billing with Microsoft; RMS owns the customer relationship and charges clients directly in NGN.

05
Week 2
Accept distributor partnership invitation

Once a distributor agrees to onboard us, they send a one-click partnership invitation via email to the Partner Center Global Admin (CEO account). Accept it in Partner Center. The distributor's name then appears under "Indirect providers" — we can now place orders through their portal on behalf of clients.

→ Exit: distributor visible under Partner Center → Account Settings → Indirect Providers.
06
Week 2
Partner Center billing & FX settlement setup

Add the company bank account (or corporate card) to the distributor's billing portal. Most Africa distributors invoice monthly in USD. We are billed by the distributor; we then invoice clients in NGN at our marked-up rate. The domiciliary account is the settlement instrument — keep a 30-day USD reserve to absorb FX timing risk.

→ CFO note: reconcile distributor invoices to client billing each month-end. Build the FX variance into pricing — do not absorb it.
07
Week 2
Complete Microsoft business & identity verification

Microsoft conducts business verification: company name vs CAC records, email domain ownership, and employment/identity verification of the primary contact (CEO). Have CAC certificate, utility bill (office address), and BVN ready. Verification status appears in Partner Center → Account Settings → Legal Info. If not verified within 5 business days, contact Partner Center support.

08
Week 2
Sign Microsoft Partner Agreement & go live

Final acceptance of the MPA in Partner Center. Once accepted, the CSP transaction surface activates: we can quote a client, place an order with the distributor, and provision licences directly into the client's tenant within minutes. First paying client should follow within 10 business days of this milestone.

→ Day-1 sales motion: the first three clients are existing RMS contacts. We do not chase strangers in the first month — we convert the easiest five conversations in our address book.
