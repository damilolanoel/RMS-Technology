02 — Strategic Rationale

Why this, why now.

Six independent reasons converge on the same answer. Each one alone would justify the pivot. Together they make it inevitable.

📈
Revenue Quality

Research is project-by-project — one client, one study, gone. Tech is recurring. A client on M365 Business Standard pays every month. MRR is worth 5–8× project revenue at exit.

🏗️
Infrastructure Advantage

We already hold the TD Africa partnership, the Microsoft Partner Center pathway, and the commercial relationships most new tech companies take 2–3 years to build. We are activating an asset, not starting from zero.

🌍
Market Timing

Nigeria's SME sector is undergoing forced digitisation — CBN cashless policy, NDPR enforcement, remote-work needs. The market is creating a buyer's urgency that did not exist three years ago.

💰
Margin Structure

Licensing margins are 10–22% on zero-COGS recurring revenue. Hardware via TD Africa is 8–18% pass-through. Managed IT services run 65–80% gross. The fully-built tech model out-margins research significantly.

🎯
Simpler to Sell

Research is intangible — you sell a methodology and a promise. Tech is tangible: "here is your laptop, here is your licence key, here is your IT dashboard." Lower trust barrier, faster cycle, easier to prove value.

🔒
Stickiness

Managed-tech clients churn at 8–12% annually vs 40–60% for project research clients. A company whose team data lives in RMS-configured Azure has high switching costs by design.

⚠️
On the research business — the hard truth

The research business is not abandoned — it is quietly wound down as the tech business scales. We honour existing commitments and convert research clients who are also natural tech buyers. Purely-research clients are referred to peer agencies. We stop bidding new research, stop building research capability, and stop presenting ourselves as a research firm from this document forward.

05 — Business Model

Three revenue engines.
One client relationship.

Not one business line — three distinct, complementary revenue streams that compound over time. Each engine serves the same client in a different dimension, and each engine feeds the next.

Engine 01
Software Licensing
// MRR — Recurring

Microsoft 365 (all SKUs), Azure consumption, Copilot, Dynamics 365, Defender. Provisioned monthly, invoiced monthly. Every client onboarded keeps paying every month. At 100 M365 Business Standard seats at ₦8,500/seat/month, that is ₦850k/month in MRR before you lift a finger.

Margin: 10–22% recurring
Engine 02
Hardware Supply
// Transactional

HP, Dell, Lenovo laptops and desktops. Cisco networking. Samsung devices. APC UPS. Bosch CCTV. Starlink. Apple. All sourced wholesale via TD Africa. Transactional and high-value but not recurring — the foot-in-the-door for software. A company that buys laptops from us is a natural M365 prospect.

Margin: 8–18% per project
Engine 03
Managed IT Services
// MRR — Highest Margin

Monthly IT support retainer, endpoint management, email security management, user provisioning, helpdesk. ₦15k–₦65k per user per month depending on scope. The highest-margin, stickiest business in the model. A company on RMS-managed IT does not switch easily.

Margin: 65–80% gross
